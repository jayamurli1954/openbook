# OpenBook Contributor Protection & Attribution

## Purpose

OpenBook is built as a community-oriented open-source publishing platform. This policy explains how the project intends to recognize and protect the people who contribute code, documentation, design, publishing expertise, translations, accessibility work, testing, templates, examples and other original project material.

## 1. Open source does not mean anonymous

OpenBook welcomes contributions from individuals and organizations. Meaningful contributions should be recorded and contributors should receive appropriate recognition.

The project will maintain contributor records through Git history, release information, contributor documentation and, where appropriate, in-product credits.

## 2. Copyright remains with the contributor

Unless a contributor explicitly transfers copyright under a separate written agreement, the contributor retains copyright in original material they contribute.

Submitting a contribution does not by itself mean that the contributor transfers ownership of their copyright to SanMitra Tech Solutions.

## 3. License for accepted contributions

OpenBook's project license is Apache License 2.0. Contributions accepted into the project must be provided under terms that allow OpenBook to maintain and distribute the project consistently with its licensing model.

The exact contribution mechanism (for example, DCO or CLA) will be documented before substantial external contributions are accepted.

## 4. Attribution

OpenBook will preserve appropriate copyright and attribution information required by applicable licenses and will maintain project-level contributor recognition.

Where practical, the project may provide:

- `NOTICE` attribution;
- contributor listings;
- release acknowledgements;
- in-app credits;
- recognition for translators, designers, accessibility specialists and publishing experts; and
- links to public contributor profiles where the contributor chooses to provide them.

Contributor recognition must not be represented as a warranty, endorsement or employment relationship.

## 5. Commercial use

Apache-2.0 permits commercial use of OpenBook subject to its terms. OpenBook does not promise financial compensation merely because a third party commercially uses the project.

The project may establish separate voluntary programs in the future, such as sponsorship, grants, bounties, maintainer funding or a community contribution fund. Such programs do not change the Apache-2.0 license.

## 6. Third-party material

Contributors must not submit code, fonts, images, templates, documentation or other material that they do not have the right to contribute.

Third-party material must retain its applicable license and attribution requirements. Contributors should identify such material clearly in the pull request and relevant project records.

## 7. AI-assisted contributions

AI-assisted work is permitted where consistent with project policy. The contributor remains responsible for ensuring that the submitted contribution is suitable for distribution and does not knowingly introduce incompatible third-party material or licensing obligations.

Contributors should disclose significant AI assistance when requested by project maintainers for provenance, review or compliance purposes.

## 8. Trademark and branding

The OpenBook name, logos and other project marks are separate from copyright licensing. Apache-2.0 does not grant unrestricted trademark rights.

Commercial products may describe compatibility or incorporation accurately, but must not imply that SanMitra Tech Solutions or the OpenBook project endorses a product without permission.

## 9. Changes to this policy

This policy may evolve as the community grows. Changes must not retroactively remove rights already granted under the applicable software license.

## Core principle

> **OpenBook is free and open to everyone. Anyone can use it, improve it, build upon it or build a business around it. At the same time, OpenBook respects the people who make the project possible by preserving contribution history, copyright and appropriate attribution.**
